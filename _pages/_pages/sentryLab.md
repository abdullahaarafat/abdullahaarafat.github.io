Welcome to the Safe and Efficient Real-Time sYstems (**SENTRY**) Lab!
==
At SENTRY Lab, we develop resilient, secure, and intelligent real-time systems for safety-critical applications. Our research spans real-time scheduling, cyber-physical systems, robust learning, and embedded system security — all focused on ensuring safe and efficient system operation under uncertainty.
